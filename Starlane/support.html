Common questions

Is Magic Maps free? Browsing the map is free, and your first 3 guided trips are free. After that, Magic Maps Pro unlocks guidance and every vehicle: open the menu, then Settings, then Subscribe. You can cancel any time in your iPhone's Settings under your Apple Account, then Subscriptions.

How do I restore my subscription on a new iPhone? Open Settings in Magic Maps and tap Restore purchases.

How do I use Magic Maps in my car? Connect your iPhone to a CarPlay car. Magic Maps appears on the car's screen with search, destinations and turn-by-turn guidance.

How do I mute voice directions? While driving, tap the speaker button: next to End on the phone, at the top of the map buttons in CarPlay. Settings also has a switch for spoken directions.

Why is a road missing? Magic Maps' streets come from OpenStreetMap, which anyone can improve at openstreetmap.org.

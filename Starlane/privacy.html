Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

Magic Maps is a navigation app made by giantleap. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. This policy explains what leaves your iPhone and why.

Your location

Magic Maps uses your location to show where you are on the map and to guide you to your destination. Your location is used on your device. It is only sent off your device in these cases, and only to provide the feature you are using:

  • Directions. When you ask for a route, your starting point, any stops and your destination go through Magic Maps' routing relay (run on Cloudflare) to Stadia Maps, which calculates the route. The relay passes the request on and doesn't log or store it. To keep others from using it, your iPhone proves it is running the genuine Magic Maps app with Apple's App Attest; this uses a key created on your iPhone that identifies the app install, not you.
  • Traffic. To show traffic on nearby roads, the app asks Magic Maps' relay for live traffic in the map area around you, which it gets from TomTom. When you look at route choices, or a new route is found while driving, the relay also sends TomTom the shape of each route to learn how long it takes in current traffic. TomTom sees the relay's requests, not your iPhone, and the relay doesn't log or store them.
  • Map tiles. To draw the map, the app downloads map tiles for the area on screen from OpenFreeMap. Like any web request, this reveals the general area being viewed.
  • Stop signs and traffic lights. The app downloads these for the area on screen from public OpenStreetMap Overpass servers.
  • Search. Place search and place details use Apple's MapKit, under Apple's privacy policy.

None of these requests include your name, an account, or any identifier we create. We do not sell location data, share it for advertising, or use it to track you across apps.

Data stored on your iPhone

Your trip log, recent destinations, theme, ship and settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are not uploaded or synced. You can clear recent destinations in Settings, and deleting the app removes everything.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Manage or cancel your subscription in your iPhone's Settings under your Apple Account.

CarPlay

When connected to CarPlay, Magic Maps shows the same map and directions in your car. No car data is collected.

Children

Magic Maps is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
